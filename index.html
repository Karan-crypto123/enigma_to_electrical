ABOUT THE PLATFORM

About £nigma

⚡

Engineering is not about memorising. It's about understanding.

£nigma to €lectrical is an independent learning platform built for Electrical Engineering students.

Our goal is simple — to bring the important resources of Electrical Engineering into one organized and easy-to-use space.

From subjects and syllabus to notes, formulas, practical resources and previous-year questions, £nigma is designed to help students learn, revise and build a stronger understanding of Electrical Engineering.

OUR MISSION

Make Electrical Engineering simpler to explore, easier to understand and more organized to study.

MY JOURNEY

Student → Learner → Builder

I am a Diploma student of Electrical Engineering, currently exploring the subject beyond just textbooks and examinations.

My journey is about understanding concepts, learning new technologies and turning ideas into something useful.

WHY I BUILT £NIGMA

Less searching.
More learning.

Electrical Engineering has a huge amount of concepts, formulas, subjects and resources. Finding everything at the right time can be difficult for a student.

£nigma to €lectrical was created with a simple idea: bring useful academic resources into one organized learning space.

The goal is not just to collect information, but to make learning Electrical Engineering easier to explore and understand.
